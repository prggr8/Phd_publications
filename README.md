# PhD Research Publications – Computational Fluid Dynamics

## Overview

This repository presents a list of my peer-reviewed journal publications and international conference proceedings arising from my research in Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD), fluid mechanics, and heat transfer.

My research focused on the numerical investigation of fluid flow, drag characteristics, and heat transfer involving Newtonian and non-Newtonian fluids, including Bingham plastic and power-law fluids.

## Research Areas

- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
- Forced and natural convection heat transfer
- Drag and momentum transfer characteristics
- Non-Newtonian fluid mechanics
- Bingham plastic and power-law fluids
- Numerical modelling and parametric analysis
- Effects of geometry, orientation, and channel confinement on flow and heat transfer
